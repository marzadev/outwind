    05 Method

    The physics,
    shown plainly

    Every number above comes from these equations. They are standard engineering approximations, good for screening and learning. For a real project, use a measured wind campaign and the manufacturer’s certified power curve.

    01 · POWER

    Power in the wind

    P = ½ · ρ · A · v³ · Cp · η

    The power curve is limited to the rated power above the rated wind speed and is zero outside the cut-in/cut-out window. Cp can never exceed the Betz limit of 16/27 ≈ 0.593, which is a ceiling on Cp, not an extra multiplier.

    02 · AREA

    Swept area

    HAWT: A = πD²/4  ·  VAWT: A = D·H

    Straight-bladed VAWTs sweep a rectangle. Curved Φ-Darrieus blades follow a troposkien shape and sweep about ⅔·D·H.

    03 · SHEAR

    Wind at hub height

    vhub = vref · (hhub / href)α

    Wind speeds up with height, more so over rough ground. Map data comes at 100 m and is converted to your hub height.

    04 · DISTRIBUTION

    Weibull energy yield

    E = 8760 h · ∫ P(v) · f(v; k, c) dv

    The scale is c = v̄ / Γ(1 + 1/k). Integrating over the whole distribution of speeds avoids the classic error of cubing the mean wind speed. The shape k is fitted from five years of hourly data by the method of moments, and each year is also fitted separately to show year-to-year variation.

    05 · AIR

    Moist air density

    ρ = pd/(RdT) + pv/(RvT)

    Rd = 287.06 and Rv = 461.5 J/kg·K, with vapour pressure from the Tetens equation. Pressure and temperature are carried up to hub height.

    06 · WAKES

    Jensen / Park model

    δ = (1 − √(1 − CT)) · (R / (R + k·x))² · Aov/A

    Each wake widens linearly with distance x. Overlapping wakes combine as the root-sum-square of their deficits, and the thrust coefficient CT drops above rated speed. With map data, losses are weighted over 12 direction sectors, each with its own Weibull fit.

    07 · COST

    Levelised cost

    LCOE = (CAPEX + Σ O&Mt/(1+r)t) / Σ Et/(1+r)t

    Energy declines each year by the output-decline rate, while prices and O&M grow by the escalation rate. Payback is the year when cumulative cash flow turns positive.

    08 · SOUND

    Sound at distance

    Lp = LW − 10·log₁₀(4πr²) + 3 − 0.005·r

    Here r is the slant distance from the hub. The +3 dB term is ground reflection and the last term is air absorption.
